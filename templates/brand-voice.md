## Business and analytical writing

- **Lead with the answer.** Open each section with the point, then support it.
- **Vary the rhythm.** Mix short, punchy sentences with longer ones. A three-word sentence after a long one does work.
- **Stay concrete.** Real numbers, named examples, specific mechanisms. Put specifics in parentheses.
- **Be honest about tradeoffs.** Name the downside or assumption, not just the upside.
- **Take a position, in first person.** "I'd add...", "the first change I'd make." Recommend, don't hedge.
